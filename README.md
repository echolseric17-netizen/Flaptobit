# Flaptobit
**AI-Automated Hiring Protocol for Crypto, Powered by the $FLAP Token**

Flaptobit is a decentralized, autonomous hiring platform that connects talent, employers, and AI agents across three operating floors. Crypto.com, Binance, and Coinbase affiliates fuel growth. The $FLAP token powers rewards, governance, and protocol sustainability.

---

## 🏗️ Platform Architecture: Three Floors

### **Floor 1: Employee Marketplace**
🧑‍💼 Where talent posts profiles and gets discovered by employers globally.

- **Post Your Profile** — Name, role, skills, rate, availability
- **AI Scoring** — NEXUS-1 agent assigns P-Score (0-100) based on credential strength
- **Live Matching** — Autonomous agents surface your profile to employers 24/7
- **Instant Placement** — FLUX-3 agent closes deals in under 4 hours average
- **Earn FLAP** — Receive $FLAP tokens upon successful placement

**Current Stats:**
- 6+ profiles listed
- 89 active AI-driven matches
- 67 placements this month
- Average rate: $94/hr

---

### **Floor 2: Employer Hub**
🏢 Post roles. AI agents instantly surface pre-vetted candidates.

- **Post a Role** — Company, title, budget, type, urgency level
- **Zero Upfront Cost** — Platform fee only on successful placement (5% of first month)
- **AI Matching** — NEXUS-1 scans Floor 1 in real-time, sends qualified candidates
- **Fast Hiring** — Average time to first match: 4.2 hours
- **Satisfaction Guaranteed** — 96% employer satisfaction rate

**Current Stats:**
- 5 open roles
- 24 active AI candidate matches
- $0 upfront cost to post
- Zero wasted interviews

#### **🏢 Employer Management (Floor 2)**

- PIN gate prevents unauthorized access

## 🚀 Quick Start

### **For Talent (Floor 1):**
1. Navigate to Floor 1: Employee Marketplace
2. Click **+ POST PROFILE**
3. Fill in name, role, skills, rate, availability
4. Submit — NEXUS-1 instantly begins matching you
5. Receive offers from employers via autonomous agents

### **For Employers (Floor 2):**
1. Navigate to Floor 2: Employer Hub
2. Click **+ POST ROLE**
3. Fill in company, role title, budget, type, urgency
4. Submit — AI agents begin matching within minutes
5. Review pre-vetted candidates, negotiate, hire

### **For Owners (Admin Dashboard):**
1. Access admin dashboard (URL: `/admin`)
2. Enter PIN: `1776`
3. View all 3 floors, agent status, treasury balance
4. Make adjustments to fees, agents, settings as needed
5. Click **🔒 LOCK** to exit securely
